Why LlamaBox?

The problems with typical AI apps — and how we solve them.

🔒

Other apps:

Most AI apps send your messages to remote servers, creating a permanent data trail.

LlamaBox:

LlamaBox runs the model entirely on your phone — your chats never leave the device.

👤

Other apps:

Other apps force you to create accounts and track every interaction you have.

LlamaBox:

LlamaBox requires no signup, no email, and collects absolutely no data.

✈️

Other apps:

Typical AI assistants stop working the instant you lose internet connectivity.

LlamaBox:

LlamaBox works fully offline — on a plane, in a basement, or anywhere off the grid.

Features

Everything you need for private, on-device AI chat.

🔒

Fully Offline

Chat with AI without any internet connection; your data never leaves the device.

📸

Multimodal Vision

Point your camera or pick a photo and let AI analyze it entirely on-device.

🔊

Voice Output

Listen to AI responses aloud with built-in text-to-speech for hands-free conversations.

📦

Model Hub

Download optimized GGUF models in-app, or import your own custom weights easily.

⚡

GPU Acceleration

Offload inference to Snapdragon and Mali GPUs for dramatically faster local generation.

📊

System Monitor

Watch live RAM, CPU, and GPU usage while chatting with your local models.
