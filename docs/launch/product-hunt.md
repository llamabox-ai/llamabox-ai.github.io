# Product Hunt (when APK or Play is public)

**Tagline:** Private AI chat that never leaves your phone.

**Description (short):**

LlamaBox runs large language models entirely on your Android device using GGUF and llama.cpp. No cloud servers, no accounts, no telemetry — chat offline after you download a model.

**Links:**
- Website: https://llamabox-ai.github.io/
- Waitlist / download: https://llamabox-ai.github.io/download.html

**First comment (maker):**

Built LlamaBox because “trust us with your prompts” is not a privacy architecture. On-device inference only, CPU-only by design for the widest Android coverage. Dual-licensed AGPL + commercial. Happy to answer anything technical.
