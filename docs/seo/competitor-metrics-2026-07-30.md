## Definitions

- **DA** — Moz Domain Authority (1-100).
- **PA** — Moz Page Authority (1-100).
- **Citation Flow** — Majestic score based on link volume.
- **Trust Flow** — Majestic score based on link quality.
- **Ref. Domains** — Number of referring domains.
- **Backlinks** — Total inbound links observed.
- **KD** — Mangools keyword-difficulty score (higher = harder to rank).

## Notes

- PocketPal and LocalLlama have very low authority (DA 6) and minimal backlink profiles, making them beatable on long-tail, intent-rich terms.
- MLC.ai sits in a mid-tier authority band (DA 39) but benefits from strong GitHub and academic links.
- Reddit, GitHub and Hugging Face are ultra-high-authority domains that will dominate head terms organically; content strategy should target the gaps they leave unanswered.
