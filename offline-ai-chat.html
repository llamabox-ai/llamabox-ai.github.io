Offline chat

Offline AI chat on Android.

Chat with a local language model even when the network is off. LlamaBox keeps every conversation on your phone.

What is offline AI chat?

Offline AI chat means having a conversation with a language model without an internet connection. The model file lives on your phone, and every token is generated locally by the CPU. Once the model is downloaded, you can chat in airplane mode, underground, abroad, or anywhere the network does not reach.

Why offline AI chat matters

Most chatbots stop working the moment you lose signal. Offline AI chat keeps working. That matters for:

  • Travel and flights — useful AI in airplane mode
  • Remote and field work — no dependency on mobile data
  • Low-connectivity regions — reliable access where networks are weak
  • Privacy-sensitive situations — no data leaves the device
  • Battery and cost — no continuous cloud round-trips

How LlamaBox runs offline AI chat

  • Download a GGUF model over Wi-Fi, then disconnect
  • LlamaBox loads the model into app memory
  • Inference runs via llama.cpp on the phone CPU
  • Prompts and answers are generated locally with no network call
  • Chat history is stored in a local SQLite database

Proving it works offline

The easiest test: download a small model, enable airplane mode, and start a chat. LlamaBox will keep answering because the model is on the phone. No trick, no cached response — live local inference.

Best free offline AI chatbot for Android

If you are looking for an offline AI chatbot free option, LlamaBox is designed for Android phones. There is no metered API, no cloud account, and no subscription. The model runs on your device, so you get a private, offline AI chatbot for Android that works even with mobile data and Wi-Fi turned off.

This makes LlamaBox a strong candidate for the best offline AI chatbot category on Android: open-source, model-agnostic, and built to work on mid-range CPUs rather than only flagship devices.

Getting started

  1. Join the LlamaBox beta waitlist for APK or Play Store access.
  2. Download a small Q4_K_M model (0.5B–3B class) while connected to Wi-Fi.
  3. Toggle airplane mode and chat. The conversation is fully offline.

FAQ

What is offline AI chat?
Offline AI chat is a conversation with a language model that works without an internet connection after the model file is stored locally on the device.
Can I chat with AI offline on Android?
Yes. LlamaBox loads a quantized GGUF model onto your phone and runs inference on the CPU. Once the model is downloaded, no network is needed.
Is there a free offline AI chatbot for Android?
Yes. LlamaBox is a free offline AI chatbot for Android. The app and open-weight GGUF models cost nothing to use, and there is no subscription or API billing.
What is the best offline AI chatbot for Android?
The best offline AI chatbot for Android depends on privacy, model choice, and ease of use. LlamaBox is built for all three: fully on-device, open-source, CPU-only, and compatible with any GGUF model you download.
Is offline AI chat private?
Yes. Because inference happens on-device, prompts and answers never travel to a cloud server. Offline and private are the same architecture in LlamaBox.
What models work for offline AI chat?
Any GGUF model that fits your phone's RAM. Start with small Q4_K_M quantizations (0.5B–3B class) for smooth offline chat on most Android phones.
Does offline AI chat need an account or API key?
No. LlamaBox requires no account, no API key, and no subscription to chat offline.
How do I prove the chat is really offline?
After downloading a model, enable airplane mode and start a new conversation. LlamaBox will continue generating answers because inference is local.
